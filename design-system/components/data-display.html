Data display

Everything the app knows arrives as one of six things: a card that groups, a KPI that reads live, a tile that states a fact, a finding row that wants a decision, a pill that labels a state, and a bullet that colours it. Nothing else gets invented per page.

Rules

Every live number carries tabular figures, or the column jitters on refresh.

A number always shows its unit and its age. Neither is optional.

Severity is the bullet's job. Do not also colour the title, the row would shout twice.

A finding without an action still gets a row. Knowing there is nothing to do is a result.
