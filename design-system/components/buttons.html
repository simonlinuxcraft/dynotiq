Buttons

Four weights, and the weight is the recommendation. Accent means this is the thing to do, ghost means a reasonable alternative, quiet means the way out. Only one accent button is ever visible in a card, otherwise the ranking stops meaning anything.

Accent · the action

The label colour is derived from the accent itself, darken(accent, .90), so it keeps the hue and stays readable on every one of the four accents.

Row as button

A whole list row that opens a page. Border and padding stripped so it reads as a row, but it is a real button and keeps focus and keyboard.

Rules

One accent button per card. If two actions both feel primary, one of them is not.

A disabled button keeps its size and loses its colour, it never disappears. The gap it would leave costs more than the dead button.

Nothing destructive is ever an accent button. Destructive actions go through a confirm, not a colour.

Labels are verbs and say what happens: "Cap the journal", not "OK".
