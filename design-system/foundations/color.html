Color

A single dark scheme. Surfaces separate by a few percent of lightness, never by a border colour, and every line is white at low alpha so it survives an accent change. Colour carries state only: yellow means brand, green/orange/red mean condition. Nothing else is coloured.

Status

No traffic light. Green and orange said the same thing as grey and yellow, only louder, so the set shrank to three steps that differ in urgency, not in hue. Warn follows the accent, which means it changes with the user's choice. Backgrounds are always the same colour at 12 % alpha.

Warn accent accent waiting for a decision

Carried by a 2 px bar down the left edge of a row, not by a round dot: it runs the height of what it belongs to and stays legible at a glance without shouting.
