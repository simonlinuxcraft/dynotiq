Layout

The window is a fixed sidebar and one scrolling column. No responsive breakpoints, no panels that come and go: this is a desktop application on a machine whose screen does not change size mid-session, so the layout does not either.

Spacing

Not a strict 8 px grid. The app is dense and the small steps carry real information, so the scale runs 2, 5, 8, 12, 14, 18, 24. Anything above 24 is page-level.

Radius

Five steps, and the rule is that radius follows size: the bigger the surface, the rounder it gets. A 12 px radius on a pill would swallow the label.

Elevation

No shadows anywhere. Depth is lightness plus one hairline, which is the only approach that survives on a compositor that may or may not be drawing shadows for the window at all.

Window frame

Sidebar is 208 px, fixed. The content column takes the rest at 24 px margin and is the only thing that scrolls.

Rules

One column of content. Two columns of findings means the user has to choose where to look first.

Cards stack, they never sit side by side unless both are shorter than four rows.

Nothing animates except the live values changing. A diagnostic tool that slides its panels around looks like it is guessing.

Minimum supported window is 1100 by 700. Below that the sidebar would have to collapse, and it does not collapse.
