Rules

Clear space around the mark is one arc stroke, 6 units on the 64 grid.

The yellow tail is the only coloured part. Everything else takes the foreground colour of its surface.

Below 24 px use the single colour version, the yellow tail turns to mud at tray size.

Never recolour the tail to an alternate accent. The accent switches, the logo does not.

Never set the wordmark in caps or add a tagline inside the lockup.
